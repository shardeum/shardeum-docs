## Shardeum Ecosystem

The Shardeum ecosystem includes wallets, infrastructure, developer tools, exchanges, and applications that enable users and builders to interact with the network.

This section serves as a curated index of integrations relevant for getting started, building, and operating on Shardeum.

## What You’ll Find Here

- **Wallets** – Manage SHM and interact with the network  
- **Infrastructure** – RPC, node hosting, and backend services  
- **Developer Tools** – SDKs, APIs, and frameworks  
- **Exchanges & Onramps** – Access and move SHM  
- **dApps & Integrations** – Applications and services connected to Shardeum  

## Explore the Ecosystem

For a complete view of apps and services in the Shardeum ecosystem, visit:

https://shardeum.org/ecosystem

The documentation here focuses on selected integrations that are useful for development, testing, and integration workflows.
