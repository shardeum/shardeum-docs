title: Delegate SHM
description: Delegate SHM to validators and earn staking rewards.
---

Delegators play a crucial role in securing the network by staking their SHM tokens with trustworthy validators, without running or maintaining a node themselves. Delegators earn a portion (commission) of the validators' rewards.

## How to Delegate?

Here is the step-by-step guide to delegate your SHM tokens to a validator.

1. All staking operations are managed through the [official dashboard](https://dashboard.shardeum.org)
2. Available features include **Delegate**, **Undelegate**, and **Claim Rewards**
3. Scroll down to the Validators section. Each [validator](https://dashboard.shardeum.org/validators) displays key information, including their commission rate. Click any validator to view their full profile and decide if you want to delegate your SHM to them. *(For best practice, consider splitting your SHM delegation across multiple validators. This helps reduce the impact of validator performance issues and maximize your rewards, while also contributing to the network’s overall decentralization.)*
4. After selecting a validator, click **Delegate** on the right side of the validator’s row.
5. Enter the amount of SHM you want to delegate, then click **Delegate** to confirm the transaction.
6. Once delegated, you can view or claim rewards, add more delegation, or initiate an undelegation at any time from the [Staking section](https://dashboard.shardeum.org/staking) of the dashboard. 

**Note**: Undelegation is the process of withdrawing your delegated SHM from a validator. You're signaling that you want them back - whether to redelegate them to another validator or to simply hold them in your wallet. Once undelegated, your tokens enter a **21-day unbonding period**, during which they are locked and not earning rewards. After the 21 days are complete, the SHM returns to your wallet.
